# Privacy Policy - Obsidian Drive Bridge

Last updated: 2026-10-20

Obsidian Drive Bridge is a personal tool used only by its owner to sync notes between an Obsidian vault and the owner's own Google Drive.

## Data we access

The app accesses only files in the owner's own Google Drive that are needed for syncing, using the Google Drive API.

## How data is used

Data is used only to sync the owner's notes. It is not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party. It is not used for advertising.

## Storage

Notes and credentials are stored only on the owner's own devices and in the owner's own Google Drive. The developer does not operate any server that collects or stores user data.

## Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
